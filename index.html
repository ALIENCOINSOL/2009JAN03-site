// CHAPTER 001 — THE ORIGIN

Every revolution has a first moment.

On January 3, 2009, the Bitcoin Genesis Block — Block 0 — marked the beginning of the Bitcoin blockchain. Hidden within its coinbase transaction was a message drawn from a newspaper headline of that day.

2009JAN03 is a community token on Solana, launched on December 26, 2025 as a tribute to that history. It is not Bitcoin, and it is not affiliated with Bitcoin's creator. It exists to celebrate the origin story.

JANUARY 3, 2009 / BLOCK HEIGHT 0

The message embedded in Bitcoin's Genesis Block became an enduring piece of blockchain history — a timestamp of the world in which it was created.
